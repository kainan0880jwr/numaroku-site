Numaroku

Track what you spend on your oshi. Numaroku is an iPhone app for recording and looking back on the money you spend on your oshi — the idols, characters, VTubers, and actors you support. Tickets, merch, travel, streams, tips: see the total for all your oshis and the breakdown for each one at a glance.

Download on the App Store

Free to start (in-app purchases available). No ads. Unlimited oshis.

Using Numaroku? See Support for help and how to contact us.

Features

Log it in seconds

The number pad opens right away. Enter the amount and pick your oshi. Split a purchase between several oshis, and let recurring spending such as fan club fees be logged automatically. You can also open the logging screen from Control Center, the Action button, or Siri.

See the total for all your oshis

This month's and this year's totals, with charts per oshi and per category. "Days as a fan" appears on Home and in widgets, and budgets notify you at 80% and 100%.

Events and tickets in one place

Add concerts and shows to see the days until the next one. Track tickets from applied to lottery result to paid, with reminders on result dates and payment deadlines.

Share recap images

Monthly recap images with your oshi ranking, and a yearly recap every December. Choose portrait for stories or square for posts, and hide amounts when sharing.

Your data stays yours

Your data is stored only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The developer has no servers and never receives your records. No ads, no third-party analytics. Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID.

Free

Unlimited oshis, logging, totals and breakdowns, events and tickets, monthly recap images, the photo gallery, iCloud sync, automatic backups (including photos), CSV export and the app lock are all free. Photos: 1 per entry on the free plan. No ads. One-Tap Log, Privacy mode, skipped purchases, receipt reading and the spending calendar are free too.

Premium

Premium adds every yearly recap card (and saving them all at once), up to 100 photos per entry, oshi × category breakdowns, comparison with last year and monthly averages, budgets per oshi, the "This Month's Spending" widget, oshi color app icons, and recap images without a watermark. Premium also adds the Oshi Photos widget, the original currency and amount for spending paid abroad, adding events and deadlines to your iPhone calendar, and skipped purchases broken down by oshi and by month.

Plans: yearly, monthly, or a one-time purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store. First-time subscribers to the yearly plan get a 7-day free trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged to your Apple Account when the trial ends, and the plan then renews every year. Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. The one-time purchase is a single payment with no renewal and unlocks Premium for as long as the app is offered.
