Numaroku

Track what you spend on your oshi. Numaroku is an iPhone app for recording and looking back on the money you spend on your oshi — the idols, characters, VTubers, and actors you support. Tickets, merch, travel, streams, tips: see the total for all your oshis and the breakdown for each one at a glance.

Free to start (in-app purchases available). No ads. Unlimited oshis.

Using Numaroku? See Support for help and how to contact us.

Features

Log it in seconds

The number pad opens right away. Enter the amount and pick your oshi. Split a purchase between several oshis, and let recurring spending such as fan club fees be logged automatically. You can also open the logging screen from Control Center, the Action button, or Siri.

See the total for all your oshis

This month's and this year's totals, with charts per oshi and per category. "Days as a fan" appears on Home and in widgets, and budgets notify you at 80% and 100%.

Events and tickets in one place

Add concerts and shows to see the days until the next one. Track tickets from applied to lottery result to paid, with reminders on result dates and payment deadlines.

Share recap images

Monthly recap images with your oshi ranking, and a yearly recap every December. Choose portrait for stories or square for posts, and hide amounts when sharing.

Your data stays yours

Your data is stored only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The developer has no servers and never receives your records. No ads, no third-party analytics. Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID.

Premium

Premium adds oshi × category breakdowns, comparison with last year, budgets per oshi, all yearly recap cards, up to 100 photos per entry, the "This Month's Spending" widget, oshi color app icons, and recap images without a watermark.

Plans: yearly (first-time subscribers get a 7-day free trial), monthly, or a one-time purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store. Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
